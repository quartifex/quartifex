First release of `@quartifex/sleeve`: labels wrapped around straight or tapered bands with coverage and seam control, UVs that follow the flat die-cut, the die-line as SVG, decals placed by angle and height, and layered gloss, satin, matte, foil and spot-varnish finishes, with a three.js binding (`/three`) and an R3F component (`/react`).
