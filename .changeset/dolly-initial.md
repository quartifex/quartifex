First release of `@quartifex/dolly`: a scroll camera rig with JSON keyframes on a centripetal Catmull-Rom spline, easing per segment, chapters, frame-rate independent damping, portrait FOV compensation via safeframe, three.js glue (`/three`), a React Three Fiber hook (`/react`) and a Blender camera-path exporter.
