First release of `@quartifex/freight`: an opinionated glTF-Transform preset with a verdict. Naming rules with optional repair, dedupe, prune, weld, opt-in join, texture caps and re-encoding (WebP, AVIF, KTX2 through toktx), Meshopt or Draco, a budget pass or fail, a Markdown report and a typed React Three Fiber module. Node runner and CLI (`/node`, `freight`) and an in-browser runner (`/browser`).
