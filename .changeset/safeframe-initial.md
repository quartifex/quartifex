First release of `@quartifex/safeframe`: art-directed staging per aspect bucket. Focal-point crops for image sequences that keep the subject whole, text zones that stay clear of it, `drawFrame` for canvas, an exact camera fit for 3D with portrait field-of-view compensation, per-bucket camera keyframes, and a React hook (`/react`).
