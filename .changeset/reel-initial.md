First release of `@quartifex/reel`: an image-sequence scrub engine reading rushes manifests, with resolve-chosen tiers, AVIF or WebP, Save-Data-aware buffering, createImageBitmap decoding (optionally in a worker), cover or contain on a DPR-aware canvas, and the poster under reduced motion. React (`/react`) and GSAP ScrollTrigger (`/gsap`) adapters.
