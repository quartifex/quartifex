First release of `@quartifex/understudy`: a quality governor with a contract (WebGL, then image sequence, then poster). Starting rung and quality from the device, frame-rate adaptation of pixel ratio, shadows, post-processing and particles with hysteresis, demotion on a lost context or at the bottom of the ladder, a reasons log, a React hook and `<Ladder>` (`/react`), an R3F `<Governor>` (`/r3f`), the hand-off to reel (`/reel`) and detect-gpu through resolve (`/gpu`).
