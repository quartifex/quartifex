## Interaction and motion

### Controls feel physical

- **Press**: a control scales to 0.96 in 160 ms on an easing that starts fast and settles without overshoot (`cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1)`), and a pressed control sinks (an inset shadow). Releasing returns it the same way, and a press can be interrupted.
- **Feedback in words**: every change that matters is also said in text (a notice on the screen and a polite live-region message), never by motion alone.
- **Sound**: synthesised cues (a tick on press, a rising pair to confirm, a low pulse for SOS). **Off by default**; a passenger turns them on from the quick bar.
- **Targets**: at least 44 × 44 px everywhere; 76 px on the driver's screen while moving.

### Motion on this page

- The camera moves only with the visitor's scroll, slowly, along one path (exterior, round to the door, into the cabin).
- Changing seat folds the panel into its screen, flies the camera (1.1 s), then grows the next screen out (a GSAP Flip).
- **Reduced motion** (the system setting, or the page's own): no camera flights; the camera cuts between poses, the seat switcher cuts, and everything stays usable.

### Seat-mode transitions

Switching a screen between parked and moving reshapes the driver's layout at once (no animation to watch while driving): two large tiles, the next turn and what is playing, with locked items marked and a reason given.
