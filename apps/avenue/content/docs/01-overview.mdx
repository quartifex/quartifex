## Overview and principles

AVENUE is an infotainment system for the whole car. The driver, the co-pilot and each rear passenger get a screen with a mode of its own, and the screens share one system: what is playing, the route, the cabin's climate and what one seat sends to another.

It began as a rear-seat system for luxury cars, researched with rear-seat passengers and A/B tested. The rear-seat modules on this page come from that work. The driver and co-pilot modes extend it, and have been researched and tested with users too.

### Principles

1. **Every seat is first class.** The rear screens match the quality of the front, because the research found passengers in the back reaching for their own tablets.
2. **One system, many modes.** Each seat sees the same state in the form that suits it. Nothing is designed for one screen alone.
3. **The driver's eyes stay on the road.** While the car moves, the driver's screen shows only what can be read at a glance and hands everything else to voice, physical controls or another seat.
4. **Nothing takes over a screen uninvited.** Sharing asks the receiving passenger first.
5. **Physical feel, honest feedback.** Controls press, confirm and say what happened, in words as well as motion.
6. **Readable at night, readable in sun.** Dark first, with every pair of colours meeting WCAG 2.2 AA.
