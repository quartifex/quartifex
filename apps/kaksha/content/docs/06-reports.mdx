## Reports and export

- **Report card**: from a student's page, **Report card → Print or save as PDF**. It lists every test with marks and percentage, strengths and areas to work on, and your notes, with lines to sign for the teacher and a parent.
- **Class summary**: from a test's results, **Class summary**. Skill figures and every child's marks and group on one page.
- **Spreadsheet**: **Spreadsheet (CSV)** downloads every child's answers, marks, percentage, group and skill scores. It opens in Excel, Google Sheets or LibreOffice, Hindi names included.

PDFs are made by your phone's own print feature (“Save as PDF”). That keeps Hindi text correctly joined and selectable, needs no extra download, and works offline. Nothing is uploaded.
