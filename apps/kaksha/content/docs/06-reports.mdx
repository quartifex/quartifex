## Report cards and export

- **Report card**: from a student's page, tap the print icon. Under **What goes on the card**, choose the card's language (Hindi or English, whatever language the app is in) and what to include: strengths and areas to work on, your notes, attendance on test days, lines to sign. Then **Print or save as PDF**.
- **All report cards**: from a class, open **Reports**, then **All report cards**. Every child's card, one per page, in one PDF.
- **Class summary**: from a test's results, the print icon. Skill figures and every child's marks and group on one page.
- **Spreadsheet**: on a test's Overview, **Spreadsheet (CSV)** downloads every child's answers, marks, percentage, group and skill scores. It opens in Excel, Google Sheets or LibreOffice, Hindi names included.

PDFs are made by your phone's own print feature (“Save as PDF”). That keeps Hindi text correctly joined and selectable, needs no extra download, and works offline. Nothing is uploaded.
