## Getting started

1. Open **kaksha.quartifex.com/app** once while you have internet. The app saves itself on the phone as it loads.
2. Install it (see *Put it on your phone* above) so it opens from your home screen like any other app.
3. **Add a class**: the school, the class or grade, the section and the year.
4. **Add students**: import a list, paste from a spreadsheet, or type them in one by one.
5. **Make a test**: an answer key (A to D, or right and wrong) or a rubric, or start from one of the examples.
6. **Mark**: tap each child's answer. The app moves on by itself.

**Working offline.** After the first visit, nothing in the app needs the internet: not opening it, not marking, not reports or backups. A small piece of code called a service worker keeps a copy of the app on the phone and serves it from there. When we publish a new version, the phone fetches it the next time it is online, and switches to it once every copy of the app is closed, so a marking session is never interrupted.

**Keep your data safe.** Your marks live only in this phone's browser. If the phone is lost, reset, or the browser's data is cleared, they are gone. Make a backup now and then (*Backup and moving to a new phone*, below).
