## Reading results

A test's results have four tabs.

- **Overview**: the class average, how many children are marked, and how the class scored, in five bands of marks. **Worth a look** names the questions fewer than half got right, and the skill where most children are below half. The line under it says what the figures are based on: only children with every answer marked. Absent children are named and left out, never counted as zero.
- **Questions**: the share of the class that got each question right. Orange is below half. Where many children chose the same wrong answer, it says which. Tap a question to see what every option drew and who chose the most common wrong answer: it is often one shared mix-up worth going over.
- **Skills**: the share of marks the class earned in each skill, and the change since the last test in the same subject.
- **Groups**: the class ranked by score and split into four groups. Children with equal scores are always in the same group. Shown from four children up. Below them, the children below half in each skill, lowest first.

**A class's Progress tab** shows the average of every test over time, subject by subject, and skills added up across tests. **Students** lists the class with each child's latest result; search it, or filter it to those below half or often absent.

**A student's page** shows their latest result and how it has changed, what they are strong in and what to work on, every test, and your notes about them (only on this phone).

**Insights** looks across tests: who sat each test, children below half in two tests in a row, and skills across everything marked so far, for one class or all of them.

The rules behind every number (rounding, ties, absent children) are written down in the code, in `src/lib/scoring.ts`, and checked by tests.
