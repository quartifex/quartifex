## Reading the class and student views

**Class view of a test** (**Results**):

- **Class average** and **attendance** for the test.
- **What it is based on.** Figures use only children with every answer marked. Absent and unfinished children are listed, and left out, never counted as zero.
- **By skill**: the share of marks the class earned in each skill tag.
- **By question**: the share of the class that got each question right. Bars below half are marigold. Where many children chose the same wrong answer, it is pointed out: it may be a common misunderstanding.
- **Quartile groups**: the class ranked by score and split into four groups. Children with equal scores are always in the same group. Shown from four children up.
- **Who needs help, by skill**: children below half in a skill, lowest first.

**Progress** on the class page shows the average and attendance of every test over time.

**A student's page** shows their results over time for each subject (absences appear as gaps, not zeros), and their strengths and gaps by skill, added up across their finished tests.

The rules behind every number (rounding, ties, absent children) are written down in the code, in `src/lib/scoring.ts`, and checked by tests.
