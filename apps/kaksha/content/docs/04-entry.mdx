## The marking screen

This is where the time goes, so it is built for one thumb in a noisy room.

**First, who is here.** A new test opens with the class list. Tap anyone who was absent, then **Start marking**. Absent children are skipped while you mark and left out of class figures, never counted as zero. You can still mark someone absent later.

- **By question** (the default): you see question 1 and the first child. Tap A, B, C or D, and the next child comes up. After the last child, question 2 starts. This is fastest when you go through a pile of answer sheets one question at a time.
- **By student**: one child at a time, every question in turn. Best when you have one sheet in your hand.
- **Left blank** records that the child did not answer (it scores zero). **Absent** marks the child absent for the whole test.
- **Undo** steps back through your last taps, one at a time, and puts you back where you were.
- The strip under the name shows the whole row or column. Tap any box to jump to it. **See all marks** opens the full grid of every child and question; tap any box there to change it.
- Every tap is saved on the phone at once. Close the app in the middle, or let the battery die: when you come back, **Today** offers to continue where you stopped.
- On a computer, the keys **A–D** (or **1–4**), **R** and **W**, **space** for blank, **X** for absent, **Z** to undo and the **arrow keys** all work.

**Rubrics** show one child at a time with every criterion and its level descriptors. Tap a level for each criterion and add a short note if you like; the note appears on the report card.
