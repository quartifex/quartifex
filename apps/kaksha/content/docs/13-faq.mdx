## Questions

**Is it really free?** Yes, for every teacher. If we ever offer paid help to large networks (custom rubrics, training), the app itself stays free.

**Do you see my students' marks?** No. They never leave your phone unless you share a file yourself.

**What if my phone is lost?** Your data is lost with it unless you have a backup. Make one every week or two, and keep it somewhere other than the phone.

**Can two teachers share a class?** Not live. Each can send a backup, and one phone can combine them (*Backup and moving to a new phone*).

**Will it work on my phone?** It is built for inexpensive Android phones (2 GB of memory) as well as iPhones and computers, in Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox.

**Is this an official government or board tool?** No. It is an independent, open-source tool. The example templates are examples, not official curricula.

**Where is the code?** On GitHub, under the MIT licence: [github.com/quartifex/quartifex](https://github.com/quartifex/quartifex/tree/main/apps/kaksha). Anyone can read it, check it, or run their own copy.
